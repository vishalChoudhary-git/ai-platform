# AI Engineer Roadmap — ₹40–60L → ₹1Cr+ Career Track

## Purpose

This is the **career and engineering skill roadmap**, separate from the Lead AI Engineer interview preparation.

The goal is to progress from strong backend + GenAI application engineering to **Senior / Lead / Staff-level Production AI Engineering** and eventually target ₹1Cr+ through high-value employment, international opportunities, consulting, and/or AI products.

## How to use this roadmap

For each skill:

```text
Learn concepts
    ↓
Implement a small example
    ↓
Implement it in an AI use case
    ↓
Integrate it into the AI Platform / Document Intelligence work
    ↓
Study production trade-offs
    ↓
Measure quality / latency / cost / reliability
    ↓
Document the design
```

```text
        ↓
Lead / Staff AI Engineer
        ↓
International compensation / Consulting / AI Product
        ↓
₹1Cr+ annual income potential
```
